Cluster Root Ensemble

Cluster Root is an experimental music ensemble based on Whadjuk Noongar boodja in Boorloo (Perth, Western Australia). Cluster Root performs and commissions the work of Western Australian composers, as well as presenting repertoire from the experimental tradition.

Cluster Root acknowledges the Whadjuk people of the Noongar nation as the custodians of the land on which we are based.
